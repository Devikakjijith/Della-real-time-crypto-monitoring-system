What is Della
Your Personal AI-Powered
Crypto Intelligence Platform

Della is a real-time cryptocurrency monitoring and intelligence platform built to help users make informed decisions in the fast-moving digital asset market. By combining two powerful machine learning models — an LSTM neural network and an XGBoost classifier — Della analyses live price data and technical indicators to generate clear BUY, SELL, and HOLD signals for the top cryptocurrencies. It is not just a price tracker; it is a complete decision-support system.

What Della Does
Six Powerful Tools,
One Dashboard

Della provides live price monitoring for 10 cryptocurrencies sourced directly from the Binance API, refreshing every minute. For the top 5 coins — Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, BNB, and XRP — it runs an ensemble of two AI models that analyse 13 technical features to predict short-term price direction. Users can build a personal watchlist, set custom price and signal alerts that trigger real-time email notifications, view interactive Chart.js price and volume charts, read the latest filtered crypto news, and explore deep analytics through a live Grafana dashboard backed by InfluxDB time-series data.

Importance of Crypto
Cryptocurrency is
Reshaping the World

The global cryptocurrency market has grown from a niche experiment into a multi-trillion dollar asset class. Over 420 million people across the world now own or actively use cryptocurrency. Bitcoin alone has outperformed every major traditional asset class over the last decade. Blockchain technology is transforming banking, payments, supply chains, healthcare records, and digital ownership. Countries are launching central bank digital currencies, major corporations are adding Bitcoin to their balance sheets, and an entire decentralised finance ecosystem has emerged that operates without traditional intermediaries. Crypto is no longer a fringe movement — it is a fundamental part of the global financial infrastructure being built for the next generation.

The Challenge
Markets Move Faster
Than You Can Watch

Unlike stock markets that close at 5 PM, crypto trades 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, across every time zone on the planet. Bitcoin can move 10% in a single hour. A single tweet, a regulatory announcement, or a large whale transaction can trigger massive cascading price swings before most investors even open their phones. Research shows that over 80% of retail crypto traders lose money — not because cryptocurrency is inherently dangerous, but because they lack access to real-time data and actionable intelligence. Emotional decisions, delayed information, and the sheer complexity of technical analysis overwhelm most individual investors who are trying to navigate the market alone.

How Della Helps
Intelligence You Can
Actually Act On

Della solves the information problem. Instead of spending hours reading charts and calculating indicators manually, users get a clear signal — BUY, SELL, or HOLD — generated by two independently trained AI models that agree through a weighted ensemble vote. The LSTM model learns sequential price patterns across 60 hours of market history, while XGBoost evaluates 13 snapshot features including RSI momentum, MACD crossovers, Bollinger Band position, ATR volatility, and volume ratio. When the models agree, confidence is high. When they disagree, the system defaults to HOLD — the safest and most conservative outcome. Coupled with instant email alerts, live charts, and a curated news feed, Della puts institutional-grade monitoring tools directly into the hands of every user.

Why It Matters
Democratising Access
to Smart Investing

Professional trading firms spend millions of dollars building algorithmic systems, hiring quantitative analysts, and running server farms to gain a fraction of a second's edge in the market. Individual investors have never had access to the same quality of tools — until now. Della bridges that gap by bringing machine learning based signal generation, real-time data pipelines, and automated alert systems together into a single, accessible web platform. Whether you are a student exploring crypto for the first time or an experienced trader looking for a smarter monitoring layer, Della gives you the clarity to act with confidence rather than anxiety.
